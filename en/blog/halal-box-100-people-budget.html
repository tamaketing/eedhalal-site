"How much should a 100-person meeting budget for halal meal boxes?" is the question procurement officers and event organisers ask before they request budget approval. A meeting, training day, or seminar of 100 people is a sizeable spend, so the number needs to be clear from the start. This article covers reference budgets per head, a table that shows the total for 100 people, what makes this order good value, and how to get a real price instead of a guess.

Why this matters

The figures in this article are reference prices published on the EED HALAL website. Final pricing depends on the menu, quantity, and event theme — never submit an approval with a number you guessed. Always request a written quotation before confirming an order.

How much should you budget per head for a 100-person meeting?

The easiest way to think about it is "budget per head × 100" — one number is enough to set the total. EED HALAL groups budgets into three tiers based on the menus you can choose from:

Budget per head Menus you can pick Total for 100 people (approx.)
60 THB Standard menus, e.g. stir-fried chicken with holy basil, chicken over rice ~6,000 THB
70–80 THB A wider standard selection, including beef-protein menus ~7,000–8,000 THB
90–120 THB+ Premium menus, e.g. khao mok gai, Arabic-style beef ~9,000–12,000+ THB

Simple math: 60 THB × 100 people = 6,000 THB, 70 × 100 = 7,000 THB, 80 × 100 = 8,000 THB, 90 × 100 = 9,000 THB, and 120 × 100 = 12,000 THB. These are net prices, no VAT charged (we are not VAT-registered) — the final price is stated clearly on the quotation our team sends you.
