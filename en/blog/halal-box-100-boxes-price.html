How far in advance should you order 100 boxes?

A 100-box order is cooked fresh every day, with no MSG, under CICOT halal standards (certification CICOT HL-2024-0892) — the kitchen needs lead time to deliver on schedule:

• Orders of 50–100 boxes — recommended 5–7 business days in advance
• Orders above 100 boxes — recommended 1–2 weeks in advance

For every size, confirm the quantity, menu, and delivery time before 15:00 on the business day before delivery.
