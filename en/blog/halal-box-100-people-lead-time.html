Halal Meal Box for 100 People: How Many Days in Advance?

Planning Updated August 13, 2026 | For HR / Procurement / Event Coordinators
Article Summary

Orders of 50–100 halal meal boxes should be placed 5–7 business days in advance; orders beyond 100 boxes recommend 1–2 weeks; orders of 10–50 boxes need 2–3 business days. Every order must be confirmed — menu, quantity, and delivery time — before 15:00 on the business day before delivery.

A 100-person event is not just a bigger meeting — it is a much larger kitchen workload with a lead time that must be planned correctly. This article answers the direct question "how many days in advance should I order for 100 people?" with a lead time table based on EED HALAL's published terms.

Why this matters

The real deadline is not "delivery day" but "15:00 on the business day before delivery" — the last moment to confirm and amend an order. Order late, and you risk sold-out menus and settling for substitutes.

Lead time table for halal meal box orders

EED HALAL sets lead times by quantity so the kitchen can source ingredients and schedule capacity properly:

Boxes Recommended lead time Best for
10–50 boxes 2–3 business days Small meetings, training days, team meals
50–100 boxes 5–7 business days Large single-day or multi-day meetings
100+ boxes 1–2 weeks Multi-day seminars, events

Planning 1–2 weeks ahead for a 100-person event gives you two advantages: menus stay available, and the kitchen and documents are fully ready from your first quotation.

The deadline to remember: 15:00 on the business day before delivery

All order sizes can be reserved by contacting the team at any time, but final confirmation follows the same rule: confirm the menu, quantity, and delivery time by 15:00 on the business day before delivery, and amend quantities until the same deadline.

Example: for an order on Thursday, confirm by Wednesday 15:00. Ordering 5–7 business days ahead builds exactly this buffer into your schedule — room for file updates, headcount changes, and confirmations. This is why "ordering in advance" is not a guideline, but flexibility you cannot buy back once you order late.

Pro tip

For 100+ people, request a quotation 2 weeks before the event, then confirm per the table above. You lock in pricing early and stay flexible to amend quantities until 15:00 on the business day before delivery.
