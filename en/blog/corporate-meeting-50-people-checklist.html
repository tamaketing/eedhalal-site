Planning food for a 50-person company meeting? Here's your checklist

For Corporate Updated August 14, 2026 | For HR / Procurement
Article Summary

A 7-step checklist for feeding 50 people, in the order you should do things: count confirmed attendees plus a 5–10% buffer, set a per-head budget (standard menus from 60 THB/box, premium 100–150 THB+), pick 2–3 menus, check lead times, and confirm before 15:00 — an order of exactly 50 boxes gets free delivery across Bangkok.

A 50-person meeting or training day is the most popular size for mid-size companies — serious enough to matter, small enough to manage without a full events team. The decisions procurement actually has to make are few: the real number of people who will show up, the per-head budget, 2–3 menu options, and the confirmation deadline. This checklist is ordered by the timeline you work to.

Where people slip up most

Getting the headcount wrong is the number one cause of leftover or missing food — count confirmed attendees, not registrations, and add a 5–10% buffer for staff and last-minute participants. But be decisive: confirm the final number clearly before 15:00 on the business day before delivery.

7-item checklist for a 50-person company meeting

☐ 1. Count real attendees and add a 5–10% buffer
Count people who have confirmed attendance, then add a buffer for staff and walk-ins on the day — for example, 45 confirmed → book 48–50 boxes.

☐ 2. Set the per-head budget before choosing menus
Standard menus start at 60 THB/box; premium menus (khao mok gai, Arabic-style beef, premium sets) are 100–150 THB+. For 50 people the total budget lands around 3,000–7,500+ THB depending on the menu group.

☐ 3. Pick 2–3 menus with different proteins
No group of 50 ever likes the same food — pair Thai with Indian menus, or mix popular items with one premium option. Minimum per menu: 5 boxes for Thai menus and 10 boxes for Indian menus, e.g. 2 menus × 25 boxes or 3 menus × 15–20 boxes. Every box comes from a CICOT-certified halal kitchen (HL-2024-0892).

☐ 4. Check the lead time for your dates
Orders of 10–50 boxes are recommended 2–3 business days in advance, and you confirm quantity, menu, and time before 15:00 on the business day before delivery.

☐ 5. Get the documents ready for procurement
Request a written quotation — prices have no VAT charged (we are not VAT-registered) — and receive a regular receipt when you pay. That is all procurement needs to claim the expense.

☐ 6. Give precise drop-off details
Share the address, floor/meeting room, and the time window for delivery — at least 15 minutes before the scheduled lunch break.

☐ 7. Add drinks or snacks if the budget allows
Bottled water or light snacks make the meal feel complete — ask for them on the same quotation.

Quick math

If you pick a standard menu at 60 THB/box × 50 people = 3,000 THB, which already hits the free-delivery threshold of 50 boxes. Any budget left over can go toward drinks or snacks without ballooning.
