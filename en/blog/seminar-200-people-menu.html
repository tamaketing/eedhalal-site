200-person seminar: how to pick meal box menus without lots of leftovers?

Event Menu Planning Updated August 14, 2026 | For Event Coordinators / HR
Article Summary

For a 200-person seminar, base your meal box order on confirmed attendees (not the number invited), add a 5–10% buffer, and offer 3–4 easy-to-eat protein menus — chicken, beef, seafood, or Indian. Stagger delivery windows and set a clear drop point. Boxes start at 60 THB, free delivery on 50+ boxes.

At a 200-person seminar, too much food means wasted budget, and too little means a ruined event. That is exactly where menu choice and box count matter most. This article answers, straight and practically, how to order meal boxes for 200 people that are just right — no big pile of leftovers, no shortfall.

Why this matters

Have your on-site team confirm the actual headcount — don't guess from registration numbers alone. At this scale, an error of just 20–30 boxes means food thrown away, or people queuing and grabbing boxes when supply runs short.

How to choose menus and quantities that fit 200 people

Ordering "just right" is not a secret formula — it is these five rules:

1. Order from confirmed attendance, not the number invited — 200 invited does not mean 200 boxes will be picked up. Base your count on people who have actually confirmed, then add a 5–10% buffer for walk-ins who never confirmed
2. Offer 3–4 different protein menus (chicken / beef / seafood / Indian) — give people a choice. The simple analysis: menus people can't choose become the menus with the most leftovers. Spreading popularity beats 200 boxes of one single menu
3. Pick easy-to-eat menus most people enjoy — such as pad thai, holy basil stir-fry, or khao mok gai (chicken biryani) — instead of niche menus. Unusual dishes, strong-smelling or overly spicy food often comes back untouched
4. Check whether any participants are Muslim — if you are not sure, simply order halal for everyone and the matter is settled, because halal meal boxes suit all guests, not just Muslims
5. Stagger delivery into rounds, e.g. 12:00–12:30, and state the drop point clearly — include the floor, the pick-up point, and the coordinator's name. If 200 boxes arrive in one trip and distribution is slow, people crowd and grab, and the line falls apart

How far in advance should you order 200 boxes?

An order of 200 boxes falls in the 100+ group, which we recommend ordering 1–2 weeks in advance so ingredients and kitchen capacity are properly prepared (for comparison, 50–100 boxes takes 5–7 business days).
