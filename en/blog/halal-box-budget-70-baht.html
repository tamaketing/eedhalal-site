A 70 THB per-head budget is a popular figure for meetings and training days because it is easy for management to approve — and EED HALAL has standard menus that fit it exactly. This article shows which menus you can choose, and what to avoid when funds are limited.

Common mistake

Premium menus (khao mok gai, Arabic-style beef, premium sets) run 100–150 THB+ per box — above a 70 THB budget. If you want a premium touch within budget, ask the EED team about adapted standard menus instead of inflating the average.

1. Order 50+ boxes for free delivery across Bangkok
Orders of 50+ boxes get free delivery anywhere in Bangkok — your 70 THB budget then covers the food alone, leaving the full 10 THB/head buffer for drinks or snacks.

The minimum is just 5 boxes per Thai menu (10 per Indian menu), so you can offer variety at the same per-box price.

The EED team shortlists menus within budget. Free quotations are sent within 15 minutes (during business hours); Net price, no VAT charged (we are not VAT-registered), and we issue regular receipts.

Real example: 100-person event

70 × 100 = 7,000 THB per meal. With a 60 THB starting menu, more than 1,000 THB per meal remains — enough for drinks or an afternoon snack, without requesting a new budget ceiling.

What to avoid on a 70 THB/head budget

• Do not order premium menus for the whole event — keep them limited, e.g., 10–15 boxes for leadership tables
• No VAT is added — we are not VAT-registered, so the quotation price is exactly what you pay. Do not add a VAT buffer
• Do not order late — close to the event date you have fewer menu options. Plan 5–7 business days ahead for 100 people
