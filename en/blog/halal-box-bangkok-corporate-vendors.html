Bangkok has many halal meal box shops, but far fewer that genuinely handle corporate work — because corporate orders require documents, turnaround times, and consistency on top of good food. This article gives you five selection criteria and how to verify each one before you commit.

A note before you choose

Prices seen in LINE groups or online flyers guarantee nothing. Always request a written quotation under the vendor's real name, ask for the halal certificate number, and taste the food before the real event.

Five criteria for choosing a halal meal box vendor

1. Real, verifiable halal certification
The certificate must come from a recognized body such as CICOT (EED HALAL holds certificate number HL-2024-0892). Ask for the number and verify it — a halal logo on a social page is not enough.

2. Complete documents for your finance team
Quotation and a regular receipt (no VAT tax invoice), with VAT terms stated clearly. A vendor who quotes verbally but cannot produce documents is eliminated immediately.

3. Fast quotation turnaround
A corporate vendor must respond within hours, not days. EED HALAL sends quotations within 15 minutes (during business hours) with the full document set attached.

4. Transparent terms
Compare vendors on two numbers: minimum order (EED: 10 boxes) and free-delivery threshold (EED: 50+ boxes within Bangkok).

5. Capacity for large orders and on-time delivery
Ask whether the vendor can handle 100–300 boxes in one day, their recommended lead times (EED: 100+ boxes 1–2 weeks, 50–100 boxes 5–7 business days), and their latest confirmation time (EED: 15:00 on the business day before delivery).
