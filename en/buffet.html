Price and budget

How much is a halal buffet? Answered in 30 seconds

Prices start at 450 THB per guest, with a minimum of 30 guests. That price already covers halal food from every category, our team setting up the line, keeping the dishes topped up and looking after the floor for the full 4 hours of your event. Coming as a larger group? From 100 guests the per-head rate drops to 420 THB, and it keeps stepping down to 300 THB at 500 guests. Extra equipment, final capacity and transport costs are confirmed in the quotation we prepare for your event.

Per guest

From 450 THB/guest

This is the starting tier, for 30 guests. The more guests you bring, the lower the rate per head — every tier is listed below. Setup and staff costs are shared across the whole event, so bigger groups get better value

Guest count

Minimum 30 guests

For a smaller group, meal boxes or Snack Boxes usually fit better — easier to run and easier to control budget-wise

Rough budget

100 guests ≈ 42,000 THB

That is 100 × 420 THB, the rate for your tier at 100 guests — just to sense the budget. The real figure depends on the menus you choose and is set out clearly in your quotation

The more guests you host, the lower the rate per head

Once your event passes a hundred guests, the rate moves to the 100 guests 420 THB/guest tier and keeps stepping down as your group grows — bigger groups get better value because team and setup costs spread further across the event.
