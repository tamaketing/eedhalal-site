How to Order Halal Meal Boxes

Order halal meal boxes from EED HALAL in 5 steps: Choose Menu → Specify Quantity & Date → Request a Quote → Confirm Order → Receive Your Halal Meal Boxes. Message us on LINE to get started.

01

Choose Halal Meal Box Menu

Select your preferred halal meal box menu from our popular items, or let our team know your specific menu requirements.

02

Specify Halal Meal Box Quantity & Date

Indicate the number of halal meal boxes, date, time, and delivery location so our team can check availability and plan production.

03

Request a Quote

Our team will prepare a detailed quotation with all specifications and send it to you for review and approval via LINE.

04

Confirm Halal Meal Box Order

Once you approve the quote, our team will immediately start preparing ingredients and planning delivery of your halal meal boxes.

05

Receive Your Halal Meal Boxes

Our delivery team plans the delivery round so your halal meal boxes arrive before the event starts, with status updates before the event day for peace of mind.
