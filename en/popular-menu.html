Frequently asked

Still deciding? Here are the answers

What corporate customers ask us most before ordering. Anything else, just message us on LINE.

What is the minimum order?

Corporate orders start at 10 boxes. Thai and Indian menus are 10 boxes per menu. Send your headcount and budget and we will build a set that fits.

How far ahead do I need to order?

For 10–50 boxes, order 2–3 working days ahead. Confirm the count, menus, time and drop-off before 15:00 on the working day before delivery so the kitchen and drivers stay on schedule.

How is delivery charged?

Send the drop-off place and box count on LINE. Delivery is always quoted up front inside your quotation — nothing added later.

Is it really halal certified?

Yes. Certified by the Bangkok Islamic Council, no. 926/2568 (see the certificate).

How do I order?

There is no cart on this page. The fastest way is to pick the menu names you like here, then message us on LINE with the box count, date, drop-off point and budget. We check the price and send a quotation within 15 minutes during working hours.

What are the prices?

Classic from 65 THB, Signature from 160 THB and Executive Premium from 230 THB per box (food only), minimum 10 boxes per menu. Prices move with the box count — send the menu names and count on LINE and we will price it in the quotation.
