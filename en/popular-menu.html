Frequently asked

Not sure yet? Start with these answers

These are the questions corporate customers ask us most before they order. Got something else? Just send us a message on LINE.

What is the minimum order?

Corporate orders start at 10 boxes. Thai and Indian dishes are 10 boxes each. Send us your headcount and budget and we'll put together a set that fits.

How far ahead do I need to order?

For 10–50 boxes, we suggest ordering 2–3 working days ahead. Confirm the count, menus, time and drop-off by 15:00 on the working day before delivery, so the kitchen and drivers stay on schedule.

How is delivery charged?

Send us the drop-off place and box count on LINE. The fee depends on distance and how many boxes you order. We put it in your quote up front, so nothing gets added later.

Is it really halal certified?

Yes. Certified by the Bangkok Islamic Council, no. 926/2568 (see the certificate).

How do I order?

It all happens on LINE. No account to make and no long form to fill in. Press the order button on the dish you like and its name is already in the chat, so you only add the box count, the date and the drop-off point. We check the price and send a quote within 15 minutes during working hours.

What are the prices?

Classic from 65 THB, Signature from 155 THB and Executive Premium from 230 THB a box (food only), with a 10-box minimum per menu. Prices can shift with the box count. Send us the menu names and count on LINE and we'll price it in your quote.
