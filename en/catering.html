How To Choose

Which Format Suits Your Event?

Choose Bulk Pot

When you want food served in large batches for easy serving. Great for merit-making events, group gatherings, or venues without long food lines.

Choose Live Cooking Station (Fresh-Cooked Food Booth)

When you want a chef to cook fresh on-site, plate by plate, allowing guests to see the cooking process.

Brief Checklist

Information to Share with the Team Before Finalizing

  • • Event type e.g. merit-making, banquet, reception, or corporate event
  • • Estimated number of guests
  • • Date, time, and actual venue
  • • Preference for bulk pot or Live Cooking Station
  • • On-site constraints such as food setup area or building access points
