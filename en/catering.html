About Halal Catering in Bangkok

What is the minimum number of guests for halal catering?

Our halal catering service is designed for events with 100–500+ guests. For smaller groups under 100 guests, we recommend our halal meal box delivery service with a minimum of 20 boxes.

What's the difference between Bulk Pot and Live Cooking Station?

Bulk Pot (Yok Mor) serves pre-cooked food in large pots with serving staff — ideal for merit-making events and large gatherings. Live Cooking Station features chefs cooking fresh on-site, plate by plate — perfect for corporate events and occasions that need an interactive food experience.

Do you provide on-site serving staff for halal catering events?

Yes, our halal catering service includes an on-site team to manage setup, serving, and cleanup. We handle everything from booths and food lines to event themes so you can focus on your guests.

What areas do you serve for halal catering in Bangkok?

We provide halal catering across 5 main zones in Bangkok: Sathorn, Silom, Sukhumvit, Rama 3, and Ladprao. Free delivery is available for 50+ boxes within these areas.

Is your catering halal certified?

Yes, every dish we serve is 100% halal and backed by our CICOT Halal Certificate. We provide full certification paperwork for corporate events and organizational approvals.

Can you cater for 500+ guests?

Yes, our halal catering service supports events from 100 up to 500+ guests. We have experience managing large-scale banquets, corporate conferences, and special events with on-site teams to ensure smooth service.
