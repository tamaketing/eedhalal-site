How To Choose

Which Suits Your Event: Bulk Pot, Buffet, or Live Cooking Station?

Choose Bulk Pot

When you want food served in large batches for easy serving. Great for merit-making events, group gatherings, or venues without long food lines.

Choose Buffet

When you want several dishes and want guests to pick their own food. Great for receptions, corporate events, and special events. See the Halal Buffet page

Choose Live Cooking Station (Fresh Food Counter)

When you want a chef cooking fresh on site, plate by plate, so guests see the dishes being made — great for themed events and events where food is the highlight. See the Live Cooking Station page
