About EED HALAL

EED HALAL is a halal food business in Bangkok providing halal meal boxes and catering for organizations. Every box is cooked fresh daily under CICOT halal standards for meetings, seminars, and events from 10+ boxes.

WHO WE ARE

From a family kitchen to halal meal boxes for modern organizations

Thai and Indian recipes perfected over more than 40 years in the family kitchen, now serving organizations across Bangkok

EED HALAL started as a family kitchen cooking Thai and Indian food with care. Recipes built up over more than four decades were passed down to the current generation and became halal meal boxes and catering services for modern organizations.

Every box is prepared with care under CICOT halal standards, just like cooking for our own family.
