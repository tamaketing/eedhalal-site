Halal Standards

This is a very important question. The key difference lies in third-party inspection and certification:

General halal (no certificate)
The restaurant or brand claims "no pork, no alcohol" but no independent organization verifies this. Customers must rely solely on the owner's word — no verifiable evidence and no regular re-inspections.

CICOT halal (officially certified)
The Central Islamic Council of Thailand (CICOT) is a government-recognized organization with international standing. They conduct thorough inspections at every stage — ingredient sourcing, Zabiha-compliant animal slaughter, kitchen and equipment segregation, staff practices, transportation — and issue a certificate with a verifiable number. Moreover, they perform ongoing re-inspections, not just a one-time pass.

For organizations with Muslim employees or events with Muslim guests, a CICOT certificate is the most credible proof and can be attached as supporting documentation.
