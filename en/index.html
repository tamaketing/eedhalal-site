Our Story

👨‍🍳 Over 40 Years, from Eed's Small Kitchen
to Corporate Meal Boxes & Catering

From a small restaurant on Mahaesak Soi 3 to the kitchen behind many restaurants — today, EED HALAL brings the same cooking to companies and organizations across Bangkok

More than 40 years ago, a small restaurant on Mahaesak Soi 3 had a skilled cook everyone in the neighborhood knew as "Eed." When the area was expropriated for expressway construction and the restaurant had to close, Eed never stopped cooking — her kitchen became the kitchen behind restaurants across Bangkok, from special biryani to Indian and Thai dishes.

Today that story is EED HALAL — halal meal boxes and catering. The same chef, the same skill, the same care — every box prepared under CICOT Halal certification, like cooking for our own family.
